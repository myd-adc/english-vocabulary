Scope: the whole Glossa web app (auth, home, topic, review, study, connect, sheets). Mode: Operate. Phone-first PWA, iPhone, one hand; laptop secondary.
Audience/job: Ukrainian speakers capturing English words the moment they meet them, reviewing in spare minutes, browsing their own dictionary by topic.
Chosen direction: Маркер (highlighter). Memorable moment: a new word lands in the list with a highlighter swipe drawn under it in its topic's colour.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Words are kept the way you mark them while reading: a fluorescent highlighter swipe under the headword, one marker colour per topic. Refuses the grey list with one accent and the cream bookish dictionary.
OWN-WORLD: White sheet (graphite #141417 in dark), ink #18181B, one neutral zinc scale; marker inks yellow #FFE94A, pink #FF9CCB, green #8CF5A8, orange #FFC56E, blue #8FD3FF, violet #C9A7FF assigned per topic by stable hash; Onest (Cyrillic grotesque) 400–800, tight negative tracking on headings; ink-black solid buttons, 2px ink outline on the capture field, 16px radii, pill chips.
STORY: Open, type the word, see the translation land underlined in its topic marker, close. Later, browse by topic chip or review.

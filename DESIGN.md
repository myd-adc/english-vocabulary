# Glossa design system: Маркер (highlighter)

Words are kept the way you mark them while reading. Every English headword sits on a fluorescent
highlighter swipe in its topic's colour. Everything else is a white sheet, ink and one zinc scale.
Source of truth for values: `web/src/styles/tokens.css`. Product truth: `PRODUCT.md`.

## Colour
- Light: sheet `#FFFFFF`, surface-2 `#F4F4F5`, text/ink `#18181B`, text-2 `#52525B`, text-3 `#71717A`, line `#E4E4E7`, danger `#C4261C`.
- Dark: graphite `#141417`, surface-2 `#1F1F23`, text/ink `#F4F4F5`, text-2 `#A1A1AA`, line `#34343A`, danger `#FF8F86`.
- Marker inks (`--mk-*`), light / dark:
  yellow `#FFE94A` / `#857400`, pink `#FF9CCB` / `#9E2F6C`, green `#8CF5A8` / `#1C7A43`,
  orange `#FFC56E` / `#9A560C`, blue `#8FD3FF` / `#1D5F99`, violet `#C9A7FF` / `#5F3AA8`, zinc `#E4E4E7` / `#3A3A40`.
- Colour means topic, nothing else. `markerFor(topic)` (`web/src/lib/marker.ts`) maps a topic to an ink by a stable hash; no topic is yellow. Topic chips and topic rows carry the same ink as their words.
- Primary actions are solid ink (black in light, near-white in dark); never a marker colour.

## Type
- Onest (variable, Latin + Cyrillic) everywhere, via `@fontsource-variable/onest`.
- Scale: 13 / 16 / 21 / 28 / 34 / 44 px. Screen titles 34px 800 with tight negative tracking; headwords 21px 700; glosses 15–16px 400 in text-2.
- In lists rank is carried by weight, not size. Sentence case; no all-caps labels.

## Signature: the swipe
- `Mark` (`web/src/components/Mark.tsx`) draws a slightly skewed band behind the lower part of the glyphs, `box-decoration-break: clone` so it wraps.
- Used on every headword: home list, topic list, quick-add result, edit preview, flashcard, quiz; the Glossa title and the active tab label use the yellow swipe.
- A newly added word draws its swipe left to right once (~380ms ease-out); off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components
- Capture field: 2px ink outline, 16px radius, 20px input text, a 44px solid-ink `+` button inside; focus shows a 5px yellow marker ring.
- Buttons: solid ink primary, 2px-ink outline secondary, danger outlined red; 44px minimum targets, 12px radius.
- Chips: pill, surface-2 fill, a dot in the topic ink; the selected chip is solid ink.
- Lists sit on the sheet separated by hairlines; no cards-on-grey. Sheets and dialogs: 16px radius, grabber, the only shadows.

## Layout and navigation
- Phone first: bottom tab bar Слова (`/`) · Теми (`/topics`) · Повторити (`/review`), safe-area aware, hidden in a study session and on auth screens.
- ≥900px: the tab bar becomes a left rail (232px), content in a 640px column.
- Motion answers touches only (sheet open, card flip, swipe draw); respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Icon
- Ink Onest ExtraBold lowercase "g" on white with a yellow swipe; generated by `tools/make_icons.py`.
